Ballastprocurement trust layer

The disruption

A supply shock is a trust collapse before it's a logistics problem.

When the Strait of Hormuz closes, your regular suppliers can't deliver. You're forced to buy fuel and feedstock from companies you've never dealt with — fast, under pressure, wiring money to unfamiliar accounts. That scramble is exactly when fraud, gouging, and payment-diversion scams win. Ballast is the layer that tells you who you can safely buy from.

Offer triage

Every inbound offer, risk-scored on arrival.

During a shock, a procurement desk gets buried in offers from unknown sellers. Ballast scores each one the moment it lands, so your team spends its limited time on the deals worth doing — and never wires money to the ones that aren't. Tap any offer to see the breakdown.

Where the AI stops. Ballast never auto-blocks a payment. During a shock, a false positive that freezes a legitimate shipment is its own damage — so the engine advises, logs, and routes to a human. The score is an input to judgment, not a replacement for it.

Systems & architecture

What happens behind the verdict.

The same pattern I built for consumer scam detection in ScamShield, retargeted at B2B procurement: ingest whatever the seller sends, extract the claims, score them against what a shock makes suspicious, and hand a human a decision they can defend.

📥
Ingest
Email, broker chat, PDF/invoice, or pasted text. OCR pulls fields from scanned docs.
🧩
Extract
Supplier identity, price, quantity, payment terms & bank details, delivery claims.
⚖️
Score
Four risk lenses run in parallel against live market & disruption context.
🧑‍⚖️
Decide
Verdict + reasons to a human. Logged for audit & supplier reputation.

The four risk lenses

Supplier legitimacy — domain age, trade history, entity match Payment integrity — advance %, changed / offshore accounts, BEC signals Price anomaly — vs live benchmark (gouging & too-good-to-be-true) Delivery plausibility — quantity vs shortage, inspection refusal, route sanity

Stack (prototype → production)

Few-shot LLM classifier (Llama 3.3 / Claude) EasyOCR — document extraction Market price + route-status feeds Supplier reputation store (Postgres) FastAPI Audit log
Why this, for this shock. The prompt asks how businesses navigate the disruption. The disruption forces them to source from strangers at speed — and every past supply shock (Suez 2021, PPE 2020) saw supplier fraud and payment-diversion scams surge in the chaos. Ballast protects the capability that breaks first: the ability to trust who you're paying.
